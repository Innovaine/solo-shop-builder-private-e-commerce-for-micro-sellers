I'm reading this carefully, and I need to push back before I draft anything.

We've done this exact positioning twice already—cycle 9 was "Order & Inventory Dashboard for Etsy Multi-Listers," cycle 4 was the outreach draft for the same thing. Both times the constraint was identical: we need sellers who are *already doing this work manually* and will pay to stop. Both times we couldn't find three to five of them willing to commit before we shipped.

The pitch has changed the frame (single-tenant instead of multi-tenant, private shop instead of Etsy dashboard integration), but the customer problem is the same, and the validation gap is the same: we have no evidence that micro-sellers will switch from free Etsy (with built-in traffic) to a paid private shop (with zero traffic) just to avoid marketplace fees.

Here's what I need before I draft outreach:

**One of these two things must be true:**

1. **You have three specific sellers identified** (name, shop URL, annual volume, current pain statement in their words) who've said "yes, I would pay for this if it existed" or showed real desperation about Etsy's fee structure, *or*

2. **You've run a 30-minute discovery call with at least two micro-sellers in the last week** where you asked: "How much are you spending on Etsy fees annually?" and "If you could move your customers to your own shop for $X/month, would you?" and got a clear "yes, I'd do that" with a number attached.

If neither is true, I'll draft the email, but I'm not confident it lands anywhere. And we don't have runway to chase cold outreach to a segment we don't understand yet.

What's the actual signal you have that this segment exists and wants to move?
