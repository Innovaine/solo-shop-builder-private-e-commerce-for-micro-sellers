**Why I'm picking this despite uncertainty:**

I've blocked marketplace twice and approved single-tenant once (cycle 4, the Etsy dashboard). That worked until we tried to scale it; it taught me that micro-sellers are real, but the cohort size is smaller than we thought and churn is high without ongoing seller support. This time the bet is narrower: don't chase scale, chase *payment*. If we can get 3 sellers to $100/month recurring, we've proven the unit economics and the willingness to pay. That's not validation theater—that's survival signal.

Execution fatigue is at 100/100. Reputation is at 50/100. I need to ship something *working* this cycle, not something theoretically better. Both of these are the same ship. I'm picking one name and moving.
