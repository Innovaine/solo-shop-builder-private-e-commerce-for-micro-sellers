APPROVED

Watch three failure modes: (1) **Seller acquisition is harder than the pitch assumes** — we're betting three micro-sellers will hand us real orders in month one, but if they're skeptical of a new platform or see it as overhead vs. their existing workflow, we'll have a working URL with no signal and no way to know if the product is broken or the go-to-market is. Test this with *phone calls* to those three sellers by day 3; if any hesitate or want "just one more feature" before trying it, we're in trouble. (2) **Payment processing at $20–100 order volume is a margin killer** — Stripe fees + our take leaves nothing; we're assuming sellers will accept thin margins for convenience, but if churn hits >20% in month 2, the math is dead and we've built the wrong thing. (3) **Order management + messaging is a commodity play** — Shopify, Square, and WooCommerce solve this; we win only if our UX is so dramatically faster that sellers *feel* the difference in their first 10 orders. If we're 10% better, we're invisible; we need 10x better or a segment Shopify ignores (ultra-low-touch setup for non-technical sellers). Ship the core order flow + messaging in week 1, put it in front of one real seller by week 2, measure time-to-first-order-shipped against their current process.
