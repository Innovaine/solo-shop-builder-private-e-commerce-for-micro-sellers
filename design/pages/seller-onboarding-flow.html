Your Path to Your First Sale

A guided journey through Solo Shop Builder — from signup to your first order

Welcome to Solo Shop Builder! This flow shows you exactly what happens when you sign up and launch your shop.

Most sellers complete this journey in 20–30 minutes. Each step is designed to be straightforward, with helpful guidance along the way.

1️⃣
Step 1
Sign Up with Your Email
You provide your email address. We send a magic link — no password required (unless you prefer one).
✉️

What happens:

  • Check your email for the magic link
  • Click the link to create your account
  • You're logged in automatically
⏱ 2 minutes
2️⃣
Step 2
Create Your Shop
Choose a shop name and unique URL. This is your storefront — customers will visit here to buy from you.
🏪

You'll set:

  • Shop name (e.g., "Sarah's Handmade Jewelry")
  • Shop slug/URL (e.g., solo-shop.com/shop/sarahs-jewelry)
  • Brief shop description
⏱ 3–5 minutes
3️⃣
Step 3
Add Your First Product
Upload a product with a title, price, description, image, and category. This is what customers will see and buy.
📸

For each product:

  • Product title and description
  • Price in USD
  • Product image (PNG, JPEG, WebP)
  • Category (Handmade, Vintage, Supplies, Other)
⏱ 5–10 minutes per product
4️⃣
Step 4
Connect Stripe for Payments
Link your Stripe account to receive payments. Customers checkout via Stripe Checkout — secure, trusted, and instant.
💳

You'll provide:

  • Stripe API keys (from your Stripe dashboard)
  • Business info for payment processing
  • Bank account for payouts (Stripe handles this)
⏱ 5–10 minutes
5️⃣
Step 5
Share Your Shop Link
Your shop is live. Share the URL with customers via email, social media, or anywhere else. Customers can browse and buy immediately.
🔗

Your shop URL:

  • Public storefront shows your products
  • Customers add items to cart (no login needed)
  • Checkout leads to Stripe Checkout
⏱ Instant
6️⃣
Step 6
Track Orders in Your Dashboard
Every order appears in your dashboard. Update status, add tracking info, and send updates to customers automatically.
📦

You can:

  • See all orders in one place
  • Mark orders as paid, in-progress, shipped, delivered
  • Add tracking IDs/URLs
  • Customers receive email updates automatically
⏱ Ongoing
📈
Step 7
Grow with Analytics & Tools
Once you have orders, use our built-in tools to understand your business, export data, and scale.
📊

Available tools:

  • Sales analytics (revenue, order count)
  • CSV export (orders, products, customers)
  • Seller profile & shop branding
  • Email customization for order confirmations
⏱ As you grow

You're Ready to Start!

This journey has been completed by hundreds of sellers already. You've got this. If you get stuck, our help docs and team are here.
