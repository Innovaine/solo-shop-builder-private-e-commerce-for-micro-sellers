Your payment didn't go through, but don't worry. Here's how to get back on track in just a few steps.

Quick recovery steps
1

Check your card details

Verify your card number, expiration date, and CVV are correct. Even one wrong digit will cause the payment to fail.

2

Contact your bank

Call your bank's fraud department to check if they blocked the transaction for security reasons. This is common for online purchases.

3

Try again with a different card

If your primary card isn't working, try a different credit or debit card. Sometimes card-specific issues prevent payment.

💡 Alternative payment methods
If you continue to experience issues, try using a debit card, a different credit card from another issuer, or contact the seller to discuss payment options.
