Getting Started
How do I set up my first shop? ▼

To create your first shop:

  1. Sign up with your email address
  2. Choose a shop name and slug (e.g., yourshop.solo)
  3. Add basic shop information (description, logo)
  4. Add your first product and image
  5. Configure payment settings with Stripe

Your shop will be live within minutes.

Can I use my own domain instead of a shop.solo subdomain? ▼

Custom domains are not yet supported in the Starter plan. We're working on this feature for the Pro plan. For now, you can use your Solo Shop subdomain (e.g., yourshop.solo).

What payment methods do customers see? ▼

We use Stripe Checkout, which accepts:

  • Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover)
  • Digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay)
  • Local payment methods (varies by region)

You can manage payout settings in your Stripe dashboard.

What are the fees? ▼

Starter plan: $20/month (up to 100 products)

Pro plan: $50/month (unlimited products)

Plus Stripe processing fees (2.9% + 30¢ per transaction). We take 0% of your revenue—you only pay Stripe's standard rates.

Products & Inventory
How do I upload multiple product images? ▼

In the product creation form, click "Add Images" and select multiple files. Images are automatically resized and optimized. We recommend:

  • Main image: 1000×1000px or larger
  • Secondary images: 800×800px or larger
  • Format: JPG or PNG
Can I set up product variants (sizes, colors)? ▼

Yes. In product settings, add variant types (e.g., "Size" with options: Small, Medium, Large). Each variant can have its own price and inventory. Customers select their choice at checkout.

How do I track inventory? ▼

Go to Inventory Management to set stock levels per product. When stock hits zero, the product shows "Out of Stock" and customers can't purchase. Set up low stock alerts to be notified before you run out.

Can I bulk import products? ▼

Yes. Use the "Import Products" feature in the product list. You'll need a CSV file with columns: Name, Price, Description, Category, Stock. Images can be uploaded separately or referenced by URL.

Orders & Shipping
How do customers track their orders? ▼

When you mark an order as "Shipped" and add a tracking ID, the customer receives an automated email with a link to track their package. They can view status without logging in.

Can customers request returns or refunds? ▼

Not yet—you manage returns directly via email. Customers can email you about refunds, and you process them manually from the order dashboard. We're building a self-service returns feature for Pro plan soon.
