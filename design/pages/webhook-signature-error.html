🔍 What went wrong?
The webhook signature didn't match. This can happen if:
  • Your signing secret is incorrect or outdated
  • The secret doesn't match what MyFatoorah has on file
  • The request was tampered with
✅ How to Fix This
1. Verify Your Signing Secret
Check your environment variable MYFATOORAH_WEBHOOK_SECRET in your `.env` file. It must match exactly what's configured in your MyFatoorah dashboard.
2. Check the MyFatoorah Dashboard
Log in to MyFatoorah → Settings → Webhooks. Confirm the signing secret and the webhook endpoint URL are correct.
3. Restart Your Application
After updating the environment variable, restart your app so the new secret is loaded.
4. Test the Webhook
MyFatoorah has a "Send Test" button in the webhooks panel. Use it to verify the connection works.
⚡ In the Meantime
Customers who attempted payment during this time should retry. If they completed payment, contact them to confirm receipt, then manually update the order status in your dashboard.
