What Happens When an Order Comes In

1

Customer Visits Your Shop

They browse your products, filter by category, and add items to their cart.

2

They Checkout

Stripe Checkout handles the payment securely. No technical setup needed — we handle it.

3

You Get Notified

Email notification + order appears in your dashboard instantly.

4

You Mark It Shipped

Click the order, add a tracking ID, mark as "shipped." Customer gets an email with tracking.

5

Customer Gets Paid Out

Revenue transfers to your bank account via Stripe. You control when and how often.
