  • Zero friction: Seller goes from signup to live shop in under 4 minutes

  • Instant notifications: Both parties notified immediately via email
  • Customer privacy: Tracking link requires no login—frictionless order tracking
  • Mobile-first design: All flows work seamlessly on mobile (<320px width)
  • Accessibility built-in: WCAG 2.1 AA compliance across all pages
